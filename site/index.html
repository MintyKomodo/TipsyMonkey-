About us

Built on preparation, relationships and execution.

Tipsy Monkey Productions is a film production company founded by Gary Colt and Emilio Gonzalez Moreno. We take projects from concept to completion, and we handle the details that decide whether a shoot is complicated or successful.

That means finding and securing locations, obtaining permits, sourcing crew, equipment and suppliers, arranging transportation and accommodation, and keeping the schedule and budget on track once the cameras roll.

Gary Colt

Founder & Producer

The person you call when you need a production to happen.

Gary Colt is an international film production professional with hands-on experience in location management, production coordination, fixing, logistics and on-the-ground production support. With more than 30 years of experience working across international environments, Gary brings a practical understanding of what it takes to move a production from an idea and a script into a working set.

His particular expertise is the Caribbean. Gary understands the region, its locations, people, infrastructure and the practical realities of filming there. From finding and securing the right locations and obtaining permits to sourcing local crew, equipment, accommodation, transportation and suppliers, he provides the local knowledge and production support that can make the difference between a complicated shoot and a successful one....

Read full bioShow less

Gary has worked alongside international production companies and film crews, coordinating with producers, directors, department heads, local authorities, property owners, suppliers and communities. He is accustomed to working in fast-moving production environments where schedules change, problems appear and solutions need to be found quickly.

His role is often about taking care of the details that allow everyone else to concentrate on the film.

Locations. Crew. Logistics. Permits. Transportation. Accommodation. Suppliers. Local relationships. Problem solving.

Gary brings these elements together and keeps them moving.

His background in luxury hospitality and large-scale operations adds another dimension to his production experience. He has spent decades managing demanding international environments, large teams, high-profile clients and complex operations. That experience has taught him to remain calm under pressure, communicate clearly and take responsibility for getting things done.

For Gary, successful production is built on preparation, relationships and execution. He believes in being involved, accessible and solution-oriented — whether the requirement is identified months before a shoot or appears unexpectedly on location.

Fluent in English and French, and having worked internationally for decades, Gary is comfortable bridging cultures and working with international cast, crews and production teams.

Today, through Tipsy Monkey Productions Incorporated, Gary brings that experience to productions looking to film in the Caribbean, the United States and internationally — providing the local expertise, production support and practical problem solving needed to get the job done.

Emilio Gonzalez Moreno

Co-Founder & Producer

The production starts before the cameras roll.

Emilio Gonzalez Moreno is a film production professional focused on turning scripts, plans and production requirements into organized, workable shoots. With a degree in Film Production from Valencia College in Orlando, combined with hands-on production experience, Emilio brings a disciplined and practical approach to the filmmaking process.

His strength is making sure the pieces are in place before production begins — and staying on top of them once the cameras start rolling....

Read full bioShow less

Emilio specializes in pre-production planning, crew coordination, scheduling, budgeting, resource management and on-set operations. He understands that a well-run production depends on preparation, clear communication and knowing what needs to happen next.

From breaking down production requirements and building schedules to coordinating crews and resources, Emilio works to identify potential problems early and find practical solutions before they affect the shoot.

On set, he brings a calm and organized approach to what can often be a fast-moving and demanding environment. His focus is keeping the production moving while giving directors, producers, cast and crew the space to concentrate on their work.

For Emilio, production is about more than staying organized. It is about creating the structure that allows creativity to happen.

He is particularly focused on delivering productions on schedule and within budget, while maintaining a professional working environment from the first day of preparation through final wrap.

As Co-Founder and Producer of Tipsy Monkey Productions Incorporated, Emilio helps drive the company's production operations and its commitment to bringing projects from concept to completion with careful planning, efficient execution and a strong understanding of what it takes to make a production work.

What we do

Production services.

Whether a requirement is identified months before a shoot or appears on location, we find the practical solution and keep the production moving.

  • Finding and securing the right locations, and working with property owners, local authorities and communities.

  • Obtaining filming permits and working through approvals with the local authorities.

  • Sourcing local crew, equipment and suppliers, and coordinating them with your department heads.

  • Moving and housing cast and crew, arranged and managed from arrival to wrap.

  • Breaking down production requirements and building schedules and budgets before the cameras roll.

  • Crew coordination, resource management and clear communication between producers, directors and departments.

  • A calm, organized set that stays on schedule and within budget, from the first day of prep through final wrap.

  • Local knowledge and relationships across the Caribbean, with support in English and French.
